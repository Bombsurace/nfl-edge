How much should each factor matter?

Team strength (Elo) is the backbone. The eleven sliders add named, adjustable weight on top of it — QB rating differential, weather severity, extra home-field emphasis beyond what Elo already accounts for, rest advantage, divisional-game closeness, offensive / defensive EPA-per-play differentials — each team's real play-by-play efficiency, season-to-date, not just their record — sack rate allowed / sack rate generated — protection and pass rush, the closest free public data gets to pressure — and travel distance / time-zone shift — how much farther, and how many more zones, the away team crossed to be there. The same sliders drive two separate models: a moneyline win-probability model and a spread margin-of-victory model, switchable below. Each starts at the value fit from 855 real games — drag a slider and every pick, and the full 855-game backtest, recomputes instantly in your browser. Weather uses a live forecast (no API key) for upcoming outdoor games where one is reachable; where it isn't (see the forecast note below the picks), it falls back to the same neutral treatment as an indoor game rather than guessing. A separate betting bar slider at the bottom of the panel controls how big an edge a pick needs before it counts as a "YES" — raise it to see how pick volume and ROI change as you get pickier.
