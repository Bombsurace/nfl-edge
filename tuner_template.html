NFL moneyline & spread model

How much should each factor matter?

Eleven adjustable factors on top of team strength (Elo), driving both a moneyline and a spread model. Drag a slider — every pick and the full 855-game backtest update instantly.

What are the factors?
QB rating differential, weather severity, extra home-field emphasis, rest advantage, divisional-game closeness, offensive/defensive EPA-per-play differentials, sack rate allowed/generated (protection & pass rush), and travel distance/time-zone shift. Weather uses a live forecast where reachable; otherwise it falls back to neutral.

Emphasis

3 = data fit · 1 = ignore · 5 = double down.

Methodology & known limitations
QB, weather, EPA (not opponent-adjusted), sack rate (a proxy for pressure — true pressure charting isn't public data), travel time zones (standard-time offsets, no DST calendar), and the spread model's cover probability are documented approximations — see docs/DESIGN.md for exactly how each is built.
